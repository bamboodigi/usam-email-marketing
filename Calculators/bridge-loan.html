Bridge Loan Calculator

A bridge loan buys time: to reposition an asset, finish a lease-up, buy out a partner, or wait for permanent financing. The price of that time is the interest-only carry plus the points and fees paid up front. This calculator shows the monthly payment, the total cost over your hold, and an effective annual cost of capital so you can compare bridge quotes on equal footing.
Frequently asked

Are bridge loans interest-only?

Almost always. You pay only the monthly interest during the term and repay the principal at exit, when you sell or refinance into permanent debt. That keeps the carry manageable while the asset is being repositioned or stabilized.

What does a bridge loan cost besides the rate?

Origination points (a percentage of the loan paid at closing) plus third-party closing costs like legal, title, and appraisal. Points are fixed no matter how long you hold, while interest accrues monthly, so a faster exit lowers the total cost but not the up-front fees.
