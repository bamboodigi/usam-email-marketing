What happens after you introduce a client

Here's what happens after you introduce a client who owns a rental they don't live in: what we ask, what we check, and how long it typically takes.

{{#if first_name}}Hi {{first_name}},{{else}}Hi,{{/if}} we ask about the rental: what it's worth, what's owed on the first mortgage and what it rents for. We will ask what the money is for, because the loan is business purpose only: it has to go to the investment.

We also check that rent covers the payments (DSCR of 1.00 or better), a 660 credit score, all the loans on the property at or under 80% of its value (CLTV), that the client doesn't live there, and what the first mortgage says about a second lien.

A typical close is 3-4 weeks, as a lump sum or a line of credit. Typical terms: $50K to $1M, up to 80% CLTV, fixed rates from 6.99%. The rate is fixed, and the prepayment penalty is 0 to 5 years. Every loan is subject to underwriting.
