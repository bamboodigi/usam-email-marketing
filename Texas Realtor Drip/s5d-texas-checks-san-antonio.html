1. Is it a rental they don't live in? It's business purpose only: the money goes to the investment.

2. Does rent cover the payments? That's DSCR 1.00 or better.

3. Is there room under 80%? All the loans on the property, including the new one, as a share of its value (CLTV).

We also look for a 660 credit score and a property worth $100K+.

Texas home equity rules (an 80% debt cap, a 2% fee cap) are for a home the owner lives in. A rental that isn't their homestead is outside them, but our 80% CLTV cap still applies.

The loan is recorded in the county where the property sits, such as Bexar, Guadalupe or Comal. See our San Antonio page.
